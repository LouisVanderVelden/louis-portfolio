Babbelende Buren

Turning neighbourhood research into a tangible product-service system.

Project
Product Service System Design
Year
2025
Role
Researcher · Designer
← PreviousNext →

The assignment was to design a product-service system grounded in a real context rather than starting from a predetermined solution. Through field research and interviews, we explored the challenges people face while integrating into transitional neighbourhoods. The research eventually focused our challenge around bridging the knowledge gap: helping people access the information, organisations and support they need to participate in society.

We translated the research into Babbelende Buren: a product-service system that connects people with the right information and support through physical neighbourhood touchpoints, shared activities and a supporting app. The system brings different services together within one connected experience rather than treating them as separate solutions.

The app extends the system beyond the physical touchpoint, helping users discover activities, create a profile, follow their integration journey and stay connected with others.
