Yhellow started from a simple question: how can discarded materials become desirable products with a second life? The project explores how upcycling, hands-on making and storytelling can turn waste into functional bags that people genuinely want to use and keep.

Yhellow starts with discarded tarpaulin, leftover fabric and recovered components. Hands-on experiments shaped the collection, while production in social workplaces made local making possible. Collaborations with companies bring new materials and stories into the bags, connecting collection, production and brand building in an active business.

Yhellow grew beyond a one-off product into a live upcycling venture. What started as a handmade experiment became a real business with a product range, collaborations and a clear brand identity. The result is a collection of functional bags that each carry their own story while proving that discarded materials can become durable, desirable products.
