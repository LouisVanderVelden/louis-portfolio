Soda Rossa

Ferrari’s identity beyond the car.

Project
Premium soda-maker concept
Year
2025
Role
Product designer
← PreviousNext →

How can Ferrari’s identity live beyond the car? Soda Rossa explores how the brand’s sense of speed, prestige and sculptural tension could be translated into a premium soda maker—without simply copying automotive forms.

The project moved from brand DNA to product language. Ferrari’s forms, materials and emotional qualities were first decoded, then translated through sketching and digital exploration into 3D concepts. Each iteration refined the proportions, surfaces and details until the product felt recognisably Ferrari while still functioning as a believable home appliance.

Soda Rossa became a premium carbonator defined by a proud vertical stance, flowing surfaces and sharper functional details. Glossy red surfaces, dark internal elements and repeated vent-like features bring Ferrari’s character into a domestic product while keeping its function immediately understandable.
