NUMO

Healthier hybrid work, one small interaction at a time.

NUMO is a physical-digital workspace companion that helps hybrid workers build healthier movement into the workday through simple, tangible interactions.

Project
Master’s Project — Product Development
Year
2026
Role
Product Designer
Prototype ↗Thesis ↓
← PreviousNext →

Hybrid work gives people more freedom, but it also removes many of the natural cues that break up long periods of sitting. NUMO explores how a physical-digital system can turn ergonomic guidance into small, timely actions without adding more screen distraction.

Research with hybrid workers, employers and experts shaped the direction. I explored furniture, digital and physical interventions before focusing on a tangible desk companion. Functional prototypes and user testing were then used to refine the interaction, feedback and internal architecture into the final system.

The final system combines a personalised app, a tangible desk device and the workspace itself. The app handles setup, ergonomic guidance and progress, while NUMO brings frequent interactions into the physical environment through light, touch and movement.
