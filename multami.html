Multami

A redesign focused on simplifying assembly and improving how the product is built.

Project
Design for assembly
Year
2024
Role
Product Designer / Developer
← PreviousNext →

The original product worked, but its assembly involved too many separate parts and operations. The challenge was to rethink the construction so it could be assembled more logically, with fewer unnecessary steps, while preserving the product’s function and overall form.

The redesign reorganised the product around a clearer assembly sequence and more integrated parts. By designing with manufacturing and assembly in mind, the construction became simpler and easier to understand while keeping the same core product idea.
