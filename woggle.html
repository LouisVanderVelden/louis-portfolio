Woggle

A playful camera toy that captures moments without interrupting play.

Year

Children naturally move, explore and imagine while they play. Taking a photo usually interrupts that flow. Woggle explores how a camera could become part of the play itself: intuitive enough to understand quickly, but expressive enough to feel like a toy rather than another digital device.

The project started by observing how children interact with toys and translated those behaviours into form and interaction. Sketching, quick prototypes and usability testing were used to explore movement, grip and control before the concept was refined through CAD, visualisation and product identity.

Woggle combines camera functionality with physical movement and simple feedback. A joystick controls capturing, recording and zooming, while the flexible body, display and light feedback make the interaction immediate and playful. The result is a camera designed to capture spontaneous moments without pulling children out of play.
