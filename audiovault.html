AudioVault

Style meets protection.

AudioVault is a protective phone container that combines wireless charging, integrated speakers and a rotating storage mechanism in one functioning prototype.

Project
Duo project
Year
2024
Role

← PreviousNext →

How can a protective phone container do more than simply hide a device? AudioVault started with a protection brief, but evolved into an object that also had to charge a phone, play sound and move between storage and display. The challenge was integrating these functions into one compact product without making it feel overly technical or complicated to use.

AudioVault became a fully functioning prototype built around three rotating parts. The final design combines protection, wireless charging and integrated audio with a physical interaction that lets the phone move between storage and display. Building the prototype forced the construction, electronics and assembly to work physically rather than only in a render.
